# Assessment rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Points | Observable evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Prediction and inquiry question | 15 | Testable hypothesis, independent/dependent variables |
| Wiring and electrical safety | 15 | Pin mapping, levels, common ground, no 5V MCU GPIO |
| Program behavior | 20 | Builds, functions, evidence recorded |
| Abstraction literacy | 20 | Expands at least three friendly macros into exact register-level C |
| Measurement and calibration | 15 | Units, uncertainty, repeated trials, calibration where relevant |
| Documentation and reproducibility | 15 | CSV data, photograph/drawing, method, observations, limitations |

## Teacher checkpoints
- After lesson 03, student maps button and LEDs onto actual port bits and explains active-low input.
- After lesson 06, student separates blocking delay, timer interrupt, and hardware PWM.
- After lesson 09, student can explain UART pin mapping, baud rate, and backchannel jumper.
- After lesson 10, student can produce and graph CSV output with meaningful units and error caveats.
- Sensor appendices are practical investigation projects, not automatically traceable laboratory standards.
